Tablechart

for Reveal.js: Appearance

One panel at a time

If a chart is inside a fragment, it builds when that fragment appears. Here, Appearance animates the fragments.

Just staggered, no fragments

Markup

Put each figure inside a fragment with an Appearance class. No other settings are needed.

<div class="panels">
  <div class="fragment animate__fadeInUp">
    <figure class="tablechart">…</figure>
  </div>
  <div class="fragment animate__fadeInUp">
    <figure class="tablechart">…</figure>
  </div>
</div>
Reveal.initialize({
  plugins: [ Tablechart, Appearance ]
});
