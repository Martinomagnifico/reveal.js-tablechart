Tablechart

for Reveal.js: Line charts

Line chart

Each row is a point. The line builds from left to right.

<figure class="tablechart" data-chart="line">
  <figcaption>
    <b>Rainfall at the orchard</b><br><span>millimetres</span>
  </figcaption>
  <table>
    <tbody>
      <tr><th>Jan</th><td>84</td></tr>
      <tr><th>Feb</th><td>71</td></tr>
      <tr><th>Mar</th><td>76</td></tr>
      <tr><th>Apr</th><td>58</td></tr>
      <tr><th>May</th><td>61</td></tr>
      <tr><th>Jun</th><td>47</td></tr>
      <tr><th>Jul</th><td>39</td></tr>
      <tr><th>Aug</th><td>52</td></tr>
    </tbody>
  </table>
</figure>

Axis and gridlines

Add data-chart-axis for the numbers along the value axis, and data-chart-grid for a line at each of them. The scale then ends on a number of the axis.

<figure class="tablechart" data-chart="line" data-chart-axis data-chart-grid>
  <figcaption>
    <b>Rainfall at the orchard</b><br><span>millimetres</span>
  </figcaption>
  <table>
    <tbody>
      <tr><th>Jan</th><td>84</td></tr>
      <tr><th>Feb</th><td>71</td></tr>
      <tr><th>Mar</th><td>76</td></tr>
      …
    </tbody>
  </table>
</figure>

Smooth

Add data-chart-shape="smooth" to the figure. The line is a curve through every point, and never goes higher or lower than the points on either side of it.

<figure class="tablechart" data-chart="line" data-chart-shape="smooth">
  <figcaption>
    <b>Rainfall at the orchard</b><br><span>millimetres</span>
  </figcaption>
  <table>
    <tbody>
      <tr><th>Jan</th><td>84</td></tr>
      <tr><th>Feb</th><td>71</td></tr>
      <tr><th>Mar</th><td>76</td></tr>
      <tr><th>Apr</th><td>58</td></tr>
      <tr><th>May</th><td>61</td></tr>
      <tr><th>Jun</th><td>47</td></tr>
      <tr><th>Jul</th><td>39</td></tr>
      <tr><th>Aug</th><td>52</td></tr>
    </tbody>
  </table>
</figure>

Grow up

Add data-chart-animate="grow" to the figure. The line grows up from zero instead of from left to right. A chart with data-fragment still builds from left to right, because each step adds a point.

<figure class="tablechart" data-chart="line" data-chart-shape="smooth" data-chart-animate="grow">
  <figcaption>
    <b>Rainfall at the orchard</b><br><span>millimetres</span>
  </figcaption>
  <table>
    <tbody>
      <tr><th>Jan</th><td>84</td></tr>
      <tr><th>Feb</th><td>71</td></tr>
      <tr><th>Mar</th><td>76</td></tr>
      <tr><th>Apr</th><td>58</td></tr>

      <tr><th>Jul</th><td>39</td></tr>

    </tbody>
  </table>
</figure>

Annotations

Annotations work the same as in the other chart types.

Several lines

Each value column is a line. The column header is shown as the name at the end of the line.

<figure class="tablechart" data-chart="line" data-chart-prefix="lineseries">
  <figcaption>
    <b>Apples per tree</b><br><span>each</span>
  </figcaption>
  <table>
    <thead>
      <tr><th>Year</th><th>North</th><th>South</th></tr>
    </thead>
    <tbody>
      <tr><th>2016</th><td>41</td><td>30</td></tr>
      <tr><th>2019</th><td>58</td><td>39</td></tr>
      <tr><th>2022</th><td>77</td><td>52</td></tr>
      <tr><th>2025</th><td>105</td><td>61</td></tr>
    </tbody>
  </table>
</figure>

Legend

Add data-chart-legend to the figure to show the names in a legend under the chart, instead of at the end of the lines.

<figure class="tablechart" data-chart="line" data-chart-prefix="linelegend" data-chart-legend>
  <figcaption>
    <b>Apples per tree</b><br><span>each</span>
  </figcaption>
  <table>
    <thead>
      <tr><th>Year</th><th>North</th><th>South</th></tr>
    </thead>
    <tbody>
      <tr><th>2016</th><td>41</td><td>30</td></tr>
      …
      <tr><th>2025</th><td>105</td><td>61</td></tr>
    </tbody>
  </table>
</figure>

Step by step

Add data-fragment to the figure to show one point per step.

<figure class="tablechart" data-chart="line" data-chart-shape="smooth" data-fragment>
  <figcaption>
    <b>Visitors to the orchard</b><br><span>thousands</span>
  </figcaption>
  <table>
    <tbody>
      <tr><th>2021</th><td>12</td></tr>
      <tr><th>2022</th><td>18</td></tr>
      <tr><th>2023</th><td>15</td></tr>
      <tr><th>2024</th><td>24</td></tr>
      <tr><th>2025</th><td>29</td></tr>
    </tbody>
  </table>
</figure>

Several lines, step by step

With data-fragment on the figure, each step adds a point to every line.

<figure class="tablechart" data-chart="line" data-fragment>
  <figcaption>
    <b>Apples per tree</b><br><span>each</span>
  </figcaption>
  <table>
    <thead>
      <tr><th></th><th>North</th><th>South</th></tr>
    </thead>
    <tbody>
      <tr><th>2016</th><td>41</td><td>30</td></tr>
      <tr><th>2019</th><td>58</td><td>39</td></tr>
      …
    </tbody>
  </table>
</figure>

Line by line

Add data-fragment to a column header to show that whole line on a step.

<figure class="tablechart" data-chart="line">
  <figcaption>
    <b>Apples per tree</b><br><span>each</span>
  </figcaption>
  <table>
    <thead>
      <tr>
        <th></th>
        <th data-fragment>North</th>
        <th data-fragment>South</th>
      </tr>
    </thead>
    <tbody>
      <tr><th>2016</th><td>41</td><td>30</td></tr>
      <tr><th>2019</th><td>58</td><td>39</td></tr>
      …
    </tbody>
  </table>
</figure>

Point by point, line by line

Add data-fragment to every cell. The steps go down each column: one line point by point, then the next.

<figure class="tablechart" data-chart="line">
  <figcaption>
    <b>Apples per tree</b><br><span>each</span>
  </figcaption>
  <table>
    <thead>
      <tr><th></th><th>North</th><th>South</th></tr>
    </thead>
    <tbody>
      <tr>
        <th>2016</th>
        <td data-fragment>41</td>
        <td data-fragment>30</td>
      </tr>
      <tr>
        <th>2019</th>
        <td data-fragment>58</td>
        <td data-fragment>39</td>
      </tr>
      …
    </tbody>
  </table>
</figure>
